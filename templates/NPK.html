About this Calculator & Disclaimer ▼

This tool provides a basic fertilizer recommendation based on simplified models. The calculations are based on assumed NPK content and are for guidance only. Actual nutrient requirements may vary significantly due to soil organic matter, climate, and specific crop varieties.

Disclaimer: This is not a substitute for professional agricultural advice or a comprehensive soil analysis. Always consult with a local agronomist before applying any fertilizer.

  • Urea: Assumed 46% N
  • DAP: Assumed 18% N, 46% P₂O₅
  • SSP: Assumed 16% P₂O₅
  • MOP: Assumed 60% K₂O
  • Organic Compost: Assumed 1% N, 1% P, 1% K
